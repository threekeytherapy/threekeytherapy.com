## Manuvision Seminars

During these hands-on seminars we’ll explore the fundamental concepts that the Manuvision community has been researching over the past 25 years. 

Each day typically includes meditation, group sharing, training, and giving and receiving treatments.

The seminars are a space to learn, connect, and grow – both as a practitioner and a person.
They are suited for people without experience as well as seasoned professionals.
