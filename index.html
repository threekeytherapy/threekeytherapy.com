How to start

1) Intro call: a short conversation about what's going on and whether this feels right. All questions and doubts welcome.
2) One session : somatic therapy on the massage table. See if it clicks and get a sense of how I can help.
3) Three more sessions : is what most people need to work through something. From then on, it's up to you.

What does a 1:1 somatic therapy session look like?

Therapy, but without getting lost in stories.

Start from the body to feel who you are, how you react, what gets in the way, and how to let go.
