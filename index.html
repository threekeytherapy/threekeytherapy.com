How it works

1) Intro call: a short conversation about what's going on and whether this feels right. All questions and doubts welcome.
2) Somatic Therapy : session on the massage table. Release tension and get to know your patterns.
3) Three sessions : is what most people need to make a serious shift in themselves. From then on, it's up to you.
